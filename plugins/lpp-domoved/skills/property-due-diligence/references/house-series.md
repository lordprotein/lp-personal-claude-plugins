## Monolith: how to assess sound insulation

The monolithic frame itself (floor slabs 20–22 cm) dampens airborne noise between floors well. The weak point: **walls between apartments and the screed.**

| Wall infill between apartments | Noise | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Monolithic reinforced concrete wall 18–20 cm | 4–5 | Best option |
| Brick 25 cm or 12 cm on both sides | 4 | Good |
| Aerated concrete 20–30 cm | 3 | Average, depends on density (D500+) |
| Tongue and groove slabs (ПГП) 8–10 cm | 2 | Weak, frequent complaints |
| Drywall (ГКЛ) on a frame without sound insulation | 1–2 | Conversations are audible |
| Drywall (ГКЛ) with mineral wool in two layers | 3 | Average |

Additionally:
- **Screed.** A floating screed with an underlay sharply reduces impact noise (footsteps, falling objects). Its absence in a new build without finishing means that the future renovation of the upstairs neighbours will determine audibility.
- **Walls between apartments near the entrance door and ventilation shafts** are often thinner: check at the viewing.
- **Economy class monolith** often uses ПГП or thin aerated concrete for walls between apartments: noise 2–3.
- **Business class monolith** usually has reinforced concrete or brick walls between apartments: noise 4–5.
- **Monolith with aerated concrete on the outside**: external noise depends on the windows more than on the walls.

How to find out: the project declaration on наш.дом.рф (structural solutions section), residential complex forums (search «{ЖК} межквартирные стены», «{ЖК} слышимость»), resident reviews.

## How to identify the series

1. The «Серия» field in the listing or on реформагкх, dom.mingkh.ru, citywalls.ru.
2. By year and number of storeys: 5 storeys from 1960–1970 is often a khrushchyovka, 22–25 storeys from 1980–2000 is often П-44 or П-3М.
3. By facade photos: characteristic balconies and windows.
4. If the series cannot be identified, set `confidence: "low"` for the internal part of noise.

## Signs in reviews

- «Слышно, как соседи разговаривают» or «слышно телевизор» (neighbours' talking or TV is audible): insulation 1–2.
- «Слышно ремонт и шаги, но не разговоры» (renovation and footsteps are audible, but not conversations): 3.
- «Соседей почти не слышно» (neighbours are almost inaudible): 4–5.
- If the building has many reviews about years of hammer drill noise: a new build with endless renovations, a temporary minus for 1–3 years.
